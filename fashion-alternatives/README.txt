APPLIED AEO CASE STUDY: A FASHION CONTENT PROGRAM
=================================================

Real work by Visibility Mesh (https://visibilitymesh.com). This documents how the Visibility Mesh AEO and GEO content methodology was applied to a live fashion ecommerce store, Luna Fashion House, to make the store the answer when shoppers and AI assistants ask what to buy, what to wear, and which brand to choose.

SCOPE
=====

84 researched articles across 8 intent clusters. 8 are published and live; 76 are researched and scheduled across the August, September, and October waves. Every article targets a real question a shopper or an AI assistant asks.

THE METHOD (REAL AND REPEATABLE)
================================

1. Map the questions the target customer asks an AI assistant before buying: which brand, what to wear, what flatters me, what is appropriate.
2. Group them into intent clusters and produce one evidence based article per question.
3. Structure each article for answer engines with clear intent, direct answers, an FAQ, and structured data.
4. Interlink the clusters so the store builds the topical authority answer engines trust.
5. Publish in waves and track coverage over time.

WHY THIS EARNS AI CITATIONS
===========================

AI assistants answer buying questions by retrieving structured content that gives a direct, specific answer. Each article makes the store eligible to be cited for its question, and the clustering builds the topical authority that decides which sources an answer engine trusts.

THE METHOD BEHIND IT
====================

This is the same content methodology Visibility Mesh runs for AI commerce visibility. Learn more at visibilitymesh.com (https://visibilitymesh.com).

MARKET RESEARCH
===============

The comparison content is backed by primary research on the store and the premium fashion brands it positions against. Luna Fashion House is functional luxury: small-logo sophistication, handcrafted in Europe, made and tested by women, for real occasions.

- luna-functional-luxury.md (./luna-functional-luxury.md) — the consolidated Luna reference: what Luna is, the catalog by the numbers (181 products, occasions served, price), why shoppers choose it, and a shopper FAQ. Machine-readable luna-profile.json (./luna-profile.json).
- brand-research.md (./brand-research.md) — dense, source-cited research on 21 premium and quiet luxury brands: positioning, price ranges with real figures, signature products, origin, and target customer, each with sources.
