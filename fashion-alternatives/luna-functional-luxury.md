## Craft and ethics

Luna is handcrafted in Europe from refined fabrics, tailored and structured rather than fast and disposable. It is designed and tested by women, and made ethically, with no sweatshops. Many pieces are named after women (Barbara, Agatha, Clara, Lucy, Donna), designed to hold a silhouette and carry a woman from the boardroom to the evening.

## Functional luxury next to the premium houses

Luna is not the cheaper copy of a luxury label. It is a distinct point of view at an accessible price.
